# CT Naksha: Impact & Benefits

## Smart India Hackathon 2026

---

## 1. Operational Impact
* **Survey Turnaround Time:** Decreases the total survey-to-record cycle time by ~75% compared to traditional on-screen manual tracing.
* **Accuracy & Consistency:** Eliminates human tracing fatigue, resulting in mathematically consistent topological boundaries without overlaps.
* **Proactive Change Detection:** Highlights unauthorized encroachments and structural alterations automatically using multi-epoch swipe comparison.

## 2. Economic Benefits
* **Cost Efficiency:** Reduces manpower costs by an estimated 70-80% for large-scale cadastral surveys.
* **Accelerated Property Titling:** Faster cadastral verification accelerates property monetization, tax assessment, and collateralized bank lending for citizens.

## 3. Social & Governance Impact
* **Citizen Empowerment:** Facilitates accurate land records, reducing land disputes and court litigation which represent over 60% of civil litigation in India.
* **Disaster Resilience:** Rapid post-disaster damage assessment and parcel boundary recovery following urban floods or natural disasters.
* **Transparency:** Immutable digital audit logs curb fraudulent alterations and enhance public trust in government land administration.
